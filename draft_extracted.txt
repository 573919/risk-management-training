--- Slide 33 ---
Good Ranking Practice 1: Use Evidence
Ground every rank in objective, verifiable data. Avoid subjective assessments that cannot be substantiated.

--- Slide 34 ---
Good Ranking Practice 2: Validate Ranks
Risk ranks should be reviewed and challenged to ensure consistency and alignment across the organization. This calibration process prevents individual bias from skewing results.
Incorporate rank validation into your leadership and governance reviews. This creates a forum for discussion and builds collective ownership of the risk landscape.

--- Slide 35 ---
Good Ranking Practice 3: Update Ranks
Risk is not static. Ranks must be revisited and updated to reflect the current reality.
Re-evaluate ranks under two conditions:
When mitigations progress: As you implement controls, the probability or impact may decrease
When conditions change: External factors (market shifts, new regulations) or internal changes can alter a risk’s profile.
